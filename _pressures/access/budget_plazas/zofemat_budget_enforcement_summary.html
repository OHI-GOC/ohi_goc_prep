ZOFEMAT: budgets & personnel for management and enforcement

Summary compiled 13–14 July 2026 · Sources in references/sources/ · Bibliography: sources_index.md · Access-capacity indicator report: pef_semarnat_zofemat_trends.html (PROFEPA inspection funding & plazas, municipal ZOFEMAT gaps, blocked-access pathway)

Scope. The Zona Federal Marítimo Terrestre (ZOFEMAT) is the federal strip of beach and dunes between the high-tide line and 20 m inland. Mexican law guarantees public access to beaches, but use or occupation of ZOFEMAT requires federal authorization. This note summarizes who administers and enforces ZOFEMAT, what budget data exists, and what personnel numbers are publicly reported.

3. PEF by state (entidad federativa), 2026

SHCP PEF analiticos tag amounts with an EF (entidad federativa) column. Multi-year state plots (Gulf states + Jalisco) are in pef_semarnat_zofemat_trends.html; tables below are a 2026 snapshot.

How to read these figures.
  • DGZFMTAC and Mares y Costas are 100% tagged to Ciudad de México — there is no PEF “per coastal state” for the national ZOFEMAT concession office.
  • PROFEPA amounts by EF are geographic tags on the authorized budget, not a published “enforcement budget for beaches in state X.” ~94% sits under CDMX (HQ / centralized). Non-CDMX lines are small and cover all PROFEPA matters in that state, not ZOFEMAT alone.
  • Oficinas de Representación are the best true state SEMARNAT budgets (one UR per state), but they cover all SEMARNAT functions in-state, not ZOFEMAT-only.

SEMARNAT (DGZFMTAC) personnel

PEF 2026 places ~92 authorized plazas under DGZFMTAC nationally (see time-series notebook / plazas analitico); the unit’s budget is entirely CDMX-tagged (~41.9 MDP). The unit processes concession applications (fee: $3,511 MXN standard application; $702 MXN for fishing/aquaculture uses per CONAMER tramite SEMARNAT-01-002). Concession lists are published by state/municipality at the SEMARNAT transparency portal, but there is no national census of active titles.

5. Legal hooks for beach access enforcement

  • Ley General de Bienes Nacionales — ZOFEMAT is federal national property; public access/transit must not be impeded.
  • Reglamento de la Zona Federal Marítimo Terrestre — concession/destination registry; SEMARNAT administration (see downloaded PDF).
  • NOM-146-SEMARNAT-2017 — official ZOFEMAT delimitation methodology.
  • LGEEPA Art. 175 bis — mandates an Inspection & Vigilance Fund from fines; Oceana/ASF report this fund was never constituted, limiting reinvestment of penalty revenue into enforcement.

6. Implications for beach-access research

  • Enforcement capacity is thin relative to coastline. A few hundred patrols and ~440 inspection visits per year across 11,000 km implies sparse routine coverage outside tourist peaks and complaint-driven responses.
  • Budget trend is downward. PROFEPA's real budget fell sharply after 2018; proposed 2025 cuts target personnel (servicios personales).
  • Almost no PROFEPA money is booked to coastal states. In PEF 2026, ~94% of PROFEPA is CDMX-tagged; the five GoC states together are only ~1.2% of the PROFEPA total (~10 MDP) — and that is still agency-wide, not ZOFEMAT.
  • State SEMARNAT offices are larger than state-tagged PROFEPA lines (e.g. BCS oficina ~18.6 MDP vs PROFEPA BCS tag ~1.7 MDP), but they are multi-purpose, not a ZOFEMAT budget.
  • Volunteers supplement staff. CVAP extends reach but is not a substitute for salaried inspectors — only 17 committees focus on ZOFEMAT.
  • Concession administration is separate from enforcement. SEMARNAT/DGZFMTAC grants titles (central budget); PROFEPA polices compliance — budget research must cover both.
  • Municipal gates are a known enforcement target — PROFEPA explicitly lists blocking beach access and unauthorized palapas/camastros as top vacation-season violations.
