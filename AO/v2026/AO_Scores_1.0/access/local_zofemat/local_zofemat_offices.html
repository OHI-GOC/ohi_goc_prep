Local ZOFEMAT offices for GoC beach-access localities

Compiled 15 July 2026 · Updated same day · Source localities: data/GofC_access_localities.csv (N = 41) · Tables: locality lookup CSV · municipio CSV · Related federal budget note: zofemat_budget_enforcement_summary.html · Access-capacity indicator: pef_semarnat_zofemat_trends.html

Purpose. Document municipal Zona Federal Marítimo Terrestre (ZOFEMAT) capacity for the 41 beach-access study localities. Offices sit at the municipio level (not per colonia/town). The municipio CSV and the municipality profiles below are the main deliverable. The locality CSV is only a join table (locality → state / municipality codes). Each municipality profile lists the study localities you explored in that municipio.

1. How the search was conducted

  1. Mapped 41 analyst localities to 25 unique municipios via INEGI CVE_ENT / CVE_MUN.
  2. Web-searched each municipio for a dedicated ZOFEMAT dirección / coordinación / departamento, plus trámite catalogs, transparency pages, and local press.
  3. Coded yes only when a named municipal ZOFEMAT unit was found; historic SEMARNAT convenios alone were not enough.
  4. Attached state PROFEPA Oficina de Representación contacts from the Feb 2024 official directory (enforcement, not municipal admin).
  5. Date checked: 2026-07-15. Contacts change — re-verify before formal outreach.
Institutional reminder. Municipal ZOFEMAT units typically handle derechos, cleaning, anuencias, and coordination. They cannot grant federal concessions (SEMARNAT / DGZFMTAC). Inspection/sanctions for blocked beach access are PROFEPA’s remit. States have SEMARNAT/PROFEPA representation offices, not dedicated “state ZOFEMAT departments” analogous to La Paz’s municipal unit.
